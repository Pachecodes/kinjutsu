## Online App: LightX2V Studio

[**LightX2V Studio**](https://x2v.light-ai.top/) is an online AI avatar
and multimodal content production platform built on the LightX2V video
generation inference framework. With AI avatar video creation at its
core, it integrates video, image, and audio creation capabilities and
supports workflow orchestration and reuse.

### MiniMax-H3 Online Tasks

- **Text-to-Audio/Video:** generate video with audio from text prompts.
- **Image-to-Audio/Video:** generate video with audio from an input image.
- **Omni-Reference Audio/Video:** generate video with audio guided by
  reference materials.

### Quick Start

1. Open [LightX2V Studio](https://x2v.light-ai.top/), sign in, and select
   a MiniMax-H3 task.
2. Enter a prompt, upload any required materials, and configure settings.
3. Review the credit cost, submit, then preview and download the result.
